# Matías Javier Vega Paredes

**Mechanical engineering student | Experimental fluid mechanics, CFD and vehicle engineering**

I am completing the professional degree in Mechanical Engineering (*Ingeniero Civil Mecánico*) at Universidad de Chile, with graduation expected in January 2027. My work combines experimental flow measurement, numerical modelling and mechanical design. I am based in Santiago, Chile, and open to graduate engineering roles in the UK and Europe.

My 5.5-year programme comprises four years of Engineering Sciences (*Licenciatura en Ciencias de la Ingeniería, mención Mecánica*) followed by 1.5 years of professional specialisation, including a research thesis.

## Selected engineering work

| Project | My contribution | Main tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Experimental flow measurement with PIV](projects/piv/README.md) | Multi-pass image processing, flow analysis and wave-generation equipment | Python, NumPy FFT, MATLAB, Arduino/C |
| [Two-element rear-wing CFD study](projects/rear-wing-cfd/README.md) | Geometry, mesh, Fluent setup and analysis of open/closed DRS configurations | ANSYS Fluent, CAD |

The case studies describe my contribution and the scope of each project. PIV includes experimental output, processing parameters and selected Python sources. CFD includes original figures, reported coefficients and the academic report. DiWheel includes team CAD and the assembled mechanical prototype; waste-heat recovery includes the proposed piping and process diagrams. The porpoising project includes the course presentation and the evolving simulation notebook.
